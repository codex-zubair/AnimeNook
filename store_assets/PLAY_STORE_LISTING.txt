--- Full description (max 4000) ---
AnimeNook answers the hardest question of the day: "What should I watch?"

Tap one button and AnimeNook picks a random anime for you from a curated,
offline catalogue. Filter by genre, spin again until something catches your
eye, or browse the whole list. No internet, no accounts, no ads — just a
quick way to decide.

PERFECT FOR
- "I don't know what to watch tonight."
- Discovering classics you may have missed.
- Settling a group decision with a fair random pick.
- Picking a genre to match your mood.

HOW IT WORKS
- Choose a genre (or leave it on All).
- Tap "Pick for me" to get a random title.
- See the year, the animation studio, the genres and a short description.
- Tap "Spin again" for a different suggestion.
- Prefer to look around? The Browse tab lists the whole catalogue.

FEATURES
- A curated catalogue of 32 well-known anime titles.
- Filter by Action, Adventure, Comedy, Fantasy, Romance, Sci-Fi, Slice of
  Life, Sports and Mystery.
- Clean, fast, one-tap interface.
- Full catalogue view with year, studio and genres.
- Works completely offline.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
- No internet permission at all — the app cannot go online.
- Zero Android permissions requested.
- No ads, no analytics, no trackers.
- The catalogue is bundled inside the app.
- The app stores no data at all — no history, no profile, nothing.

100% ORIGINAL CONTENT
Every entry is factual (title, year, studio, genres) with a short original
description written by the developer. AnimeNook contains no video, audio,
manga or other copyrighted media, and no verbatim quotations, dialogue or
lyrics.

Open source
AnimeNook's source code is public at
https://github.com/codex-zubair/AnimeNook

Built by codex-zubair.

PERMISSIONS DECLARATION (Play Console -> App content -> sensitive perms)
========================================================================
AnimeNook declares NO Android permissions. It explicitly REMOVES the network
permissions so the app cannot go online:
  - android.permission.INTERNET            (tools:node="remove")
  - android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE (tools:node="remove")

AD_ID permission. The Play Console will not prompt for permission
declarations, and the app qualifies for the "No data collected" label.
